			All the wars on the surface have awakened the Giants who for previous Ages have been sleeping down in their lairs. And now they want to get rid of those pesky little creatures (that are always getting under their feet) and return to their sleep. Giants are enormous and mighty. Their military units are the strongest in the game. Only their low numbers prevent them from ruling the world. However, due to their size, Giants cannot hire thieves (it's not easy to steal something from your 'target' when you're 5 times bigger...). They are also the best smiths and armourers. Their weapons were more valuable than gold or manit. Perhaps their awakening will mean a strong supply of quality weapons, but who knows... Trading with Giants is not easy.
