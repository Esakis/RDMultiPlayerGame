       The Central menu is your main communication and information menu. It will appear first after logging into your Kingdom.	


	
		


		
		


		 	


	
		
		
 
	 Details - here you will find info about your economy and army. Your defence and attack powers are written here. To make gameplay easier, information concerning your present income and expenditure are also listed in here. You can also check the influence of your signed pacts.


		
		


	 News - here you can find detailed information about yours and your Alliance's military actions from the last 3 Recounts. You can analyze the actions of only your Govern or your whole Alliance. There is also a possibility to search for Kingdoms or other Alliances and see their military actions. We use this tool almost everyday so it's a good idea to get to know it well.

		
		


	 Forum - there are two forums in Red Dragon. One of them is the outer Forum (found here) and the other one is the inner one (found inside the game). The main advantage of the inner forum is that only the members of your Alliance can see your posts ("Chat" and "Alliance`s Forum"). Another advantage is that you can communicate with Emperors of other Alliances ("Emperors'") and the rest of the Red Dragon community.



		
		



	 Mail - here you can find some privacy - no one unwanted can read your messages - only the receiver and you can read them. Here we usually receive messages from our allies or enemies. It is a good idea to check it at least once a day just to see if someone has declared a war on you.



		
		


	 
	 Labyrinth - here you can find a treasure : money, resources, experience for a general or lure a dragon by using the Portal.





		
		
 	Chat - here you can talk not only about RD. A friendly atmosphere enhances using it everyday.
