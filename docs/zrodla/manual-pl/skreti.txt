       There are three kinds of goblins - wood goblins, six finger goblins and war goblins. Goblins are a militant race. They're primitive creatures, living mostly in caves or abandoned mines. War goblins serve other goblins. They're extremely strong and sturdily built.
They're basically slaves of the two remaining clans, which are constantly battling each other, amassing big armies of the war goblins. Rumors say, that both wood goblins and six finger goblins aren't able to fight by themselves any more.	


	
		





	      Goblins hate elves. They detest them and never miss an opportunity to fight them. War goblins are bred like cattle in war camps and trained for combat. Every day, they're poisoned with a strange substance that makes their own will very weak. Nonetheless, there have been few rebellions in the past, but all ended in the same way - a massacre of the rebels. However some say, that a new one is rising...

Goblins spend most of their time in their caves and are lousy workers. But their advantage over other races lies in their calendar. Their year has two more months, so you have two more turns per day compared to other races. Playing as a Goblin there is only one choice - attack, attack and attack!!
