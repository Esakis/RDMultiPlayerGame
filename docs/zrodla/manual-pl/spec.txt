    Special Buildings are the basis of your Kingdom. They influence every aspect of your activity. At the beginning you can only build Special Buildings for the basic cost of 500. The rest can be built only when you have built those before. The choice list contains only Special Buildings that can be built at the moment. If you wish to build a Special Building simply choose it from the list and confirm. Special Buildings being built are shown in the Central. The cost of these buildings isn't constant but changes with your land size. The larger your Govern is the higher the cost of these buildings. The exact equation can be found in the chapter Equations. Special Buildings are built by bricklayers.
You can not tear down any Special Buildings yourself. You can only loose them as an effect of spells (Dragon Breath or Earthquake) or thievish actions. They can be also destroyed if you are successfully attacked (and the attack is led by General Sabotage and/or consists of e0 with war machines). You can change a Special Building for another while building it. Building a SB is basically gathering 'bricks' produced by bricklayers. If you change a not finished Building to another all of the 'bricks' invested are lost.
Sometimes it is a good idea to build a SB only to about 50-70%. If you are successfully attacked you will loose land, the SB cost will decrease but the built % will rise. If it reaches 100% the SB will be finished [more...]).
