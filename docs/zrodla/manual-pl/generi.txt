      Each Alliance's member has access to this menu. It shows the present Generals on every Govern in the Alliance. Here you can find info concerning :


				 	


			
				
 
 
 
  Generals' levels and the summed influence of Mages, Thieves, Merchants, Professors, Scientists and Defenders on each Govern,


 
  Level of Commanders and their secondary ability.


 
 	


			


				 	


			
				
				
 
      In each column you can find info about each type of Generals' levels and their summed influence on the Govern. I.e. in the Thieves column 8+8+7 31,5% means that the Kingdom has got 3 General Thieves with levels 8, 8 and 7 and their summed influence is 31,5%.
