 Last Justice Palace

 

 Armageddon. The world as we know it will collapse into Eternal Chaos. Only the mightiest Alliance can build the Palace and will make Red Dragon history and become Immortal. Just to begin building this structure, your Alliance must have more than 750000 acres for the whole duration of the process. Its base price is 10 000 000. All Alliances that are attempting to build it are shown in the Central. You will have to master the game to just stand a chance of building the Palace with the rest of the world trying to stop you.

If an Alliance, building the LJP, sends an attack on anyone, the LJP is reset to 0.

If more than one Alliance builds the LJP in the same Recount, they all make a stand and try to protect the Palace, until only one is left.

The winner is the Alliance that has build 100% of the LJP is the only one with 100% of LJP during the Recount.
From this Age, the Time of Apocalypse is introduced, to make the LJP easier to be built after the 100th day of the Age.
When the Palace is finished, one Red Dragon Age ends and a new one starts. A new World emerges from the Oblivion. All Kingdoms are reset to 100 Acres.
