				      The 'War' menu is used to attack other Governs. It provides an insight into your Generals and Generals of your whole Alliance.

				      The first two panels are used to either establish an attack or join your forces to an already established attack. 'Generals' menu shows your present Generals' status. 'Alliance' menu shows present Generals' status for the whole Alliance.
 	


			
				

      You cannot attack in the first five turns each day (you can send attacks after making five or more turns). If you choose the 'War' menu before making at least 5 turns this message appears:
-Attacking is not possible in the first five turns. You also cannot attack during your protection period (which lasts for the first 10 days after registration).
