      Here you can find detailed info about your Alliance's army, thieves and mages. 




      The columns :


Kingdom/race - shows Kingdoms' names and races.


Land size - shows the present Kingdoms' land sizes.


Turns - shows the used and remaining turns on Governs.


Attack/Defence - shows maximal attack's and defence's power.


Hoplites/At home - shows the number of E0 units of a Govern and the number of units remaining home.


Elite1/At home - shows the number of E1 units of a Govern and the number of units remaining home.


Elite2/At home - shows the number of E2 units of a Govern and the number of units remaining home.


War machines/At home - shows the number of war machines of a Govern and the number of machines remaining home.


Dragons/At home - shows the number of Dragons of a Govern and the number of Dragons remaining home.


Thieves/At home - shows the number of thieves of a Govern and the number of thieves remaining home.


Strength/Towers - shows the power gained from defence towers and the number of those towers.


Military Defence - shows the present Kingdom's military defence (with races' bonuses, Generals, magic and pacts).


Magical Defence - shows the present Kingdom's magical defence (with races' bonuses, Generals, magic and pacts).


Thievish Defence- shows the present Kingdom's thievish defence (with races' bonuses, Generals, magic and pacts).
